Verification

Matching geometry proves nothing about editability. One dumb imported body matches perfectly, and a free STEP round trip already gives you that. So we check behaviour instead: we replay edits on both models and compare what comes out.

Base geometry Regenerates in the target and matches on all three metrics.
5 declared dimension changes You name the driving dimensions. We apply each one on its own, to your model and to the translation, and compare the two results.
3 held-out edits We choose these, and we open them only after the translation is frozen and its hash is recorded. A held-out result stands: no retries.
Match, all three Two-sided Hausdorff < 0.05 mm over 10,000 sampled surface points; volume within 0.1 %; centre of mass within 0.05 mm.
A failing edit Fails the whole part. We do not deliver a failed part, and we do not bill for one.
The report Every edit, both models, the three numbers per edit, pass or fail. It ships with the file.

How a job runs

01

You send the part and name the 5 driving dimensions you would change.

02

We accept or decline in writing. Our written acceptance is what starts the 48-hour clock.

03

We rebuild the tree in the target system and run your 5 changes. Then we freeze the translation, record its hash, and open the 3 held-out edits.

04

You get the target file and the verification report. A failing edit fails the part.

Alternatives

These four all work today, and three of them cost less than we do. What none of them does is take a feature history in and hand one back.

STEP export, reimport Free. You get geometry and no history, so you rebuild anything you want to edit.
FeatureWorks, or SolidWorks 2026 "Convert to Geometry" STEP into SolidWorks features. Prismatic and revolved shapes only.
Backflip AI $1.52–$10 per job, as a Fusion add-in. It takes a mesh in and gives a parametric tree out, so there is nowhere to put a history.
CADSharp $250/hr with a $1,000 minimum. Designers who know the target system redo the model by hand. Their own page counts 300k+ neutral geometry migrations against just over 1K feature-based ones since 2022.
This page $149 per part, history in and out, verified by replayed edits. Not operating yet.

Sources: cadsharp.com/cad-migration read 2026-08-11; Backflip AI general availability 2026-08-03.
